Brightside branding kit

The supplied source is for Brightside Community Homes Foundation. Its visual identity combines a deep purple wordmark with a warm pink-to-orange “70/B” symbol. The anniversary lockup uses “70+ Years of Building community,” while the broader organizational descriptor is “Non-Profit Affordable Homes Since 1952.”

Typography

Primary font: Work Sans

The website loads Work Sans in regular and italic styles at weights from 300 through 700. Use it for body copy, navigation, buttons and most interface text.

Display font: Charter

Section headings use the charter class, generally in uppercase, purple and with increased letter spacing.

Recommended hierarchy:

body,
button,
input,
nav {
  font-family: "Work Sans", Arial, sans-serif;
}

h1,
h2,
.display-heading {
  font-family: "Charter", Georgia, serif;
  color: #401E6C;
  text-transform: uppercase;
  letter-spacing: 0.08em;
}

The handwritten treatment should be reserved for the word “community” within the anniversary logo rather than used as a general website font.

Logo use
Light background: gradient symbol with purple wordmark.
Purple, dark or photographic background: gradient symbol with white wordmark.
Keep generous clear space around the logo.
Do not recolour the gradient mark arbitrarily.
The site uses a dedicated light-version logo asset in both the main and sticky navigation.
Visual style

Use a clean white canvas with deep-purple overlays, soft lavender content sections and warm gradient accents. Photography should feature real residents, communities and buildings rather than luxury-oriented stock imagery. The design system uses large photographic banners, spacious layouts and rounded feature containers with approximately 20px corner radii.

Buttons follow a clear two-level system:

Primary: donation or highest-priority action.
Secondary: applications and supporting actions.
Hover/accent: orange-to-pink gradient.

The overall tone should feel accessible, community-centred, warm, optimistic and trustworthy, emphasizing safe and secure affordable housing rather than exclusivity or luxury.
