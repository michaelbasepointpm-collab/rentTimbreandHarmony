Welcome to Harmony

A place to belong on the east side

Harmony at 1451 East 12th Avenue offers bright, efficient one-bedroom homes in one of Vancouver's most connected and characterful neighbourhoods.

Part of Brightside's Timbre & Harmony development, the building replaces the historic Edward Byers House with modern, secured rental housing built to Passive House certification standards. This ultra-low-energy design keeps homes comfortable year-round while lowering utility costs and carbon footprint.

Every home is thoughtfully laid out with a private balcony, generous storage, and abundant natural light. Residents enjoy a landscaped courtyard, garden plots, a residents' lounge, and controlled-access security, all a short stroll from the shops, cafés and festivals of Commercial Drive.

The Neighbourhood

Grandview-Woodland & The Drive

Harmony sits moments from Commercial Drive, Vancouver's beloved "Little Italy," named one of the coolest streets in the world for its cafés, independent shops, festivals and easy-going community life.

From Car Free Day and Family Movie Nights in the park to the Eastside Culture Crawl and year-round patios, The Drive brings people together. Everyday essentials, transit and green space are all within an easy walk.

  • Commercial–Broadway SkyTrain · 7 min walk

    Expo & Millennium Lines. Downtown in minutes, roughly 800 m away.

  • Clark Park · 260 m

    The closest green space, with open lawns, mature trees and summer movie nights.

  • John Hendry Park (Trout Lake) · 900 m

    Community centre, farmers market, off-leash dog area and a swimmable lake.

  • Commercial Drive shops & cafés · minutes away

    Groceries, restaurants, bakeries and services along a vibrant walkable street.
